Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect personal data when you use our website, request consultations or quotations, and use our software, infrastructure, cloud, design, printing, or IT advisory services.

Last updated: December 2025

Table of Contents

Important Notice

By using our website, submitting inquiries, or engaging our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We recommend reading this document in full to understand how we handle project, communication, and customer data.

Section 01

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, project requirements, and related documents when you fill out a contact form, request a quotation, discuss a service, or communicate with us.

Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, device information, and information about how you interact with our pages, forms, and service content.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to support site functionality, remember preferences, and understand website performance. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies, but some features or form experiences may not function optimally.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

Service Delivery

To provide, deliver, and improve our services, including consultation, proposal preparation, project implementation, technical support, training, digital printing production, billing, and service-related communication.

Communication

To communicate with you about inquiries, quotations, project timelines, support requests, invoices, and service updates. We may also send marketing information where permitted or based on your preferences.

Analytics & Improvement

To monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities related to our website and service inquiries so we can improve user experience, content relevance, and service response quality.

Section 03

Data Protection & Security

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect personal and project-related information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including access controls and operational safeguards.

Encryption

Data transmitted between your browser and our website is protected using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption. Additional security controls may apply depending on the service delivery channel used.

Section 04

Your Rights

Access & Correction

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can also request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving promotional communications from us by following the unsubscribe instructions in those messages. Service, billing, security, and project communications may still be sent when necessary.

Section 05

Policy Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Notification

For significant changes, we will provide a more prominent notice, which may include email notification or a notice on our homepage.
